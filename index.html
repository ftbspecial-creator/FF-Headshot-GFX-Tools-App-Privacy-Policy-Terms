1What we collect

This policy covers the Android app FF Headshot & GFX Tools (the "app"), owned and published by Krishna Chauhan and developed by AD_FOISAL AHMED ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to it.

We do not collect personal information ourselves and we have no servers that receive your data. The app keeps a few things in its private storage on your device:

  • your chosen language, and whether you accepted this policy and finished the welcome screens;
  • presets you saved (graphics, DNS choice) and the result of your last boost;
  • which tips you have already opened.

This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2Ads and consent

The app shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads, Google and its partners may collect your Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app details (such as model, OS version and app version) and how you interact with ads.

  • Rewarded videos are optional. Some actions (Save Preset, Boost, Apply DNS) ask you to watch a short video first. If no video is available, the action continues without one.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, we show Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can reopen it from the app's Settings screen, under "Ad privacy settings", wherever it applies.
  • Ad content. We limit ad content to a teen (T) rating and mark the app as not directed to children.

Learn more: How Google uses data in ads · Google Privacy Policy. On your device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads under Settings → Google → Ads.

3How each feature uses your device

FeatureWhat it reads or doesLeaves your device?

Sensitivity and DPIReads your phone's manufacturer, model and total RAM to suggest starting values.No
