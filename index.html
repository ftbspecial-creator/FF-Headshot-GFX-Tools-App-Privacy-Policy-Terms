The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not run servers that store your personal data.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising ID and device information.
  • Your settings stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Android app Game Tools (the "app"), published by AD_FOISAL AHMED ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The app saves a few preferences on your device only, such as your chosen language and whether you accepted this policy. This data never leaves your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use the Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, OS version and app version) and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize ads.

EEA and UK users: where required, we show a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the app provides this option.

Your controls: reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device Settings (Google → Ads). More information: How Google uses data in ads and Google Privacy Policy.

3. On-device features

  • Device information (RAM, battery, temperature, screen size) is read locally and shown on your screen. It is not sent to us.
  • Installed apps list: the Booster feature reads the list of installed apps on your device so it can ask Android to stop background processes. The list is processed on your device and is not uploaded or shared.
  • Network check: the ping test connects to public internet servers to measure response time. No personal data is attached to these requests.
  • Clipboard: when you tap "Copy", the app writes the selected text to your clipboard. The app does not read your clipboard.
  • Translation: the app uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. Language packs are downloaded from Google when you pick a language, and text is translated on your device.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

5. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with Google through the AdMob SDK, as described in section 2. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove locally saved settings, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices

  • Opt out of ad personalization in your device Settings.
  • Clear app data or uninstall at any time.
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request (see below).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.

Terms of Use

Changing device settings (such as screen density) is done at your own risk. You can restore a setting at any time by following the in-app guide.
